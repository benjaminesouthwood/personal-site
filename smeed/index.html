Model Notes

Space Components:

  • Workspace: Office buildings (ground footprint = office_sqft / floors)
  • Roads: Based on Smeed's formula for car + bus person-miles
  • Parking: One space per car commuter (adjusted for occupancy and structure height)
  • Rail: Track corridors (25ft wide) + stations (40,000 sq.ft each)

Key insight: More cars → more roads + parking → larger city center

Reference: R.J. Smeed, "The Road Space Required for Traffic in Towns", Town Planning Review, Vol. 33, No. 4, January 1963.
